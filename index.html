About Me

Your Scarborough pickleball coach

Hey, I'm Andrew! I've been playing racquet sports since I first picked up a tennis racquet at age 5, and for over 7 years now, pickleball has been my game. I'm a Level 1 Certified Pickleball Instructor, and I love helping players get better, have more fun and play with confidence.

I coach beginners through intermediates. Brand new to the game? We'll start with the basics, from grip and footwork to serves, returns and scoring, so you can jump into a game feeling ready. Already playing? We'll sharpen your dinks, drops and third shots, tighten up your positioning and build smarter strategy.

  • Fun, supportive and encouraging sessions
  • Skill-building drills matched to your level
  • Private 1-on-1 or small groups of 2 to 4 friends

FAQ

Pickleball lesson questions

Quick answers about lessons, prices and booking at Picktopia in Scarborough.

Where are the pickleball lessons held?

All lessons are held at Picktopia, 3595 St. Clair Ave. East, Scarborough, Ontario. It's an indoor facility with 9 dedicated pickleball courts.

How much does a pickleball lesson cost?

Weekdays (Mon, Tue, Wed and Fri): private 1-on-1 lessons are $80/hr from 11:30am to 4pm and $90/hr from 4pm to 10pm. Group lessons are priced as a total per hour for the whole group: $130/hr for 2 or 3 players and $150/hr for 4 players before 4pm, plus $10/hr after 4pm ($140 and $160). Weekends (Sat and Sun, 11:30am to 10pm, members' prime booking hours): $100/hr private, $150/hr for 2 or 3 players and $170/hr for 4 players. All prices are tax included.

How do I see which lesson times are open?

Check the availability calendar. Green times are open and grey times are booked. Tap a green time to text me a request with that day and time filled in. An open time is not a confirmed booking: lessons depend on the club's court availability, so I'll confirm by text once the court is secured, and only then is it booked. I keep a 30-minute break after every lesson, so the next open time is 30 minutes after a lesson ends.

How do I pay for a lesson?

Cash or Interac e-Transfer only. All hourly prices are tax included.

Do I need my own paddle or equipment?

No. Every rate is all-inclusive, so paddles, balls and court fees are all included. Just show up and play.

Are lessons good for complete beginners?

Yes. Andrew is a Level 1 Certified Pickleball Instructor who coaches beginners through intermediates. New players start with the basics: grip, footwork, serves, returns and scoring.

What days and times are lessons available?

Lessons are available Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday, between 11:30am and 10pm. Weekend lessons (Saturday and Sunday) are during members' prime booking hours and use weekend rates. Thursdays are fully booked until further notice.

How do I book a pickleball lesson?

Text 647-700-7014 with the lesson you'd like and the times that work for you, or fill in the booking form on this page and it will open a text message ready to send.
